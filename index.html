Why Playful Brands Stick in People’s Minds

Branding
Read More

Serious businesses often assume a playful brand will look unprofessional. In practice, a little personality makes you easier to remember, and being remembered is half of marketing.

Playful does not mean silly. It can be a bright accent colour, a friendly line of copy on an error page, or an illustration style that feels hand-made. The key is to stay consistent so the playfulness reads as confidence, not chaos.

Start small: pick one place where your brand can smile, test it with real customers, and build from there.

04 Aug 2023

Video on Your Website: When It Helps and When It Hurts

Web Design
Read More

A short product video can explain in thirty seconds what a page of text struggles to say. A large autoplaying background video, on the other hand, can add several megabytes to your page and slow it down on mobile.

Use video when it shows something words can’t: a product in action, a process, or the people behind the business. Keep it short, add captions, and never autoplay with sound.

Always provide a lightweight poster image so the page looks finished before the video loads.

5 Signs Your Website Needs a Redesign

Development
Read More
  1. It’s hard to use on a phone. Most visitors are on mobile now.
  2. It loads slowly. Every extra second costs you visitors.
  3. You can’t update it yourself. Simple text changes shouldn’t need a developer.
  4. It no longer matches your brand. Your business has grown; your site should too.
  5. It isn’t bringing in leads. A website should be your best salesperson.

If two or more of these sound familiar, it’s probably time for a refresh.
